Account Deletion Request Users can request deletion of their Social Saver account and associated data. To request account deletion, please send an email to: socialsaverofficial@gmail.com Include: - Your registered email address - Your username (if applicable) What will be deleted: - Account information - Subscription-related account data - User preferences Data that may be retained: - Records required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or financial compliance purposes. Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days.
